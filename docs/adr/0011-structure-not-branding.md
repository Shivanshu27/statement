# 0011 — Why imitate structure and never a real bank's branding?

**Decision.** Forge layouts imitate *structural* patterns found in real
statements: split columns, Dr/Cr suffixes, parentheses, brought/carried
forward rows. They use fictional bank names and carry no logos, colours,
addresses or other identifying presentation of any real institution.

**Because.**
- Publishing PDFs that look like a real bank's documents could be mistaken
  for, or misused as, genuine statements.
- The project's claims are about *structure*; branding adds no test value.

**What it costs.** "Works on HDFC/Chase statements" cannot be claimed. The
claim is "works on the structural family those statements belong to", which
is the honest claim anyway.
